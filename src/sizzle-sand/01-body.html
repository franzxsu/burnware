SIZZLE SAND

A kitchen where every grain is simulated. Heat moves through the pan, water boils off, oil smokes and then catches, rice soaks up water and sugar turns to caramel. Cook what the tickets ask for, or just cook.

  1. Pick an ingredient on the shelf, then press and drag in the kitchen to pour it.
  2. Tap a burner knob on the stove to set the heat. Tap the tap and the hood to turn them on.
  3. Use the scoop to move food onto the plate, bowl or mug, then press Serve.
  4. Things will catch fire. A lid or the foam puts it out. Water makes it much worse.
